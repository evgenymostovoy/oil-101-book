# Oil 101 Resources — the Book and Its Supplements

The book is the spine: sessions ground themselves in the live chapter
text, and every other source here is for citation and thin spots only —
supplements never replace the book.

## Knowledge

### The book

- [Oil 101, 2nd ed. — Morgan Downey (table of contents)](https://oil101.morgandowney.com/chapters)
  The course spine. Every unit in SYLLABUS.md deep-links to its chapter
  here. Use for: grounding every session; never quote or store its text
  in this repo.
- [Appendix 1: More on the Two Most Important Forward Markets](https://oil101.morgandowney.com/appendices/forward-markets-mechanics)
  WTI/Brent contract mechanics — NYMEX CL, ICE Brent, dated Brent, EFP/EFS.
  Use for: forward-market detail behind chapters 17–20.
- [Appendix 2: Conversion Factors](https://oil101.morgandowney.com/appendices/conversion-factors)
  Barrels, tonnes, BTU, API gravity, energy density tables. Use for:
  unit conversions in any transfer task; source for a future
  `reference/` cheat sheet.
- [Appendix 3: Perpetual Futures and the 24/7 Oil Market](https://oil101.morgandowney.com/appendices/perpetual-futures)
  Crypto-style perpetuals reaching oil. Use for: context on modern
  market structure after chapters 18–19.

### Supplementary data & references (per-unit slots)

- [EIA — U.S. Energy Information Administration](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/)
  Free U.S. and international petroleum statistics: prices, inventories,
  weekly supply/demand. Use for: real data in transfer tasks (curves,
  stocks, Cushing levels); citations for market chapters.
- [JODI — Joint Organisations Data Initiative](https://www.jodidata.org/)
  Monthly global oil production/demand/stock data by country. Use for:
  global supply-demand transfer tasks.
- [SPE PetroWiki](https://petrowiki.spe.org/)
  Peer-reviewed engineering reference. Use for: thin spots in Part One's
  technical chapters (E&P, reserves) — citation only.
- [SLB Energy Glossary](https://glossary.slb.com/)
  The industry's de-facto glossary. Use for: checking terminology when
  drafting GLOSSARY.md definitions.
- _Per-unit slots_: when a unit exposes a thin spot, add its supplement
  here under this section with a one-line "Use for:" annotation.
