# Mission: Oil 101 — From the Wellsite to the Market

## Why

A drilling & completion engineer whose professional ground is well
delivery, not the wider oil business. Two outcomes, weighted equally:
become the engineer trusted with commercial context at work — able to
trace D&C and development decisions to their market and value
consequences — and become an independent reader of the oil market,
forming macro views on prices, supply and demand, and industry direction
without borrowing anyone's analysis. Both build optionality toward
commercial and markets-adjacent roles. The bar is application to real
situations, not recall for its own sake.

## Success looks like

- Reads a forward curve, a spread, or an inventory report and explains
  unaided what it implies.
- Traces a D&C or development decision to its cost, value, and market
  consequence in a commercial team's language.
- Critiques an investment case, a company strategy, or project economics
  with market context.
- Reads OPEC+ decisions, supply disruptions, and policy news and judges
  the market impact independently.
- Every tested objective holds at instant recall on spaced retrieval —
  storage strength, not same-session fluency.

## Constraints

- 30–40 minutes per day, sometimes from a phone via cloud sessions — all
  state lives in this repo and is pushed every session.
- The book (_Oil 101_, 2nd ed.) is the spine; chapter order drives
  sequencing.
- Known ground is strictly D&C. Everything else — geology, reservoir,
  production, refining, products, markets, trading — gets full treatment.
  Fast-track eligibility is decided unit by unit, only when the learner
  flags it.
- Units: market-standard first (bbl, $/bbl, Mbpd, API gravity), with
  SI/metric equivalents as parenthetical notes where useful.

## Out of scope

- Trading execution and personal position advice — the goal is
  understanding markets, not trade recommendations.
- Petroleum engineering theory depth — owned by the sibling
  petroleum-fundamentals workspace.
- Operational D&C practice (already mastered).
